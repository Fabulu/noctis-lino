## Known limitations (honest)

- **3D view**: 9×9 tile patch around screen centre (wider view needs more tiles or lower camera)
- **Orbital view**: sp globe/starfield linked but not yet wired from live camera
- **ip_drive_mode (approach)**: wired but not called per-frame
- **svsave hidden evolution**: models fuel-cell top-up but not lithium recharge loop (OMEGA-in-recharge trap not replicated)
- **Malformed starmap records** (#3876/-0.0, #34754/NaN): not rejected (drej dead code)
- **x87 across isocalls**: the lino win32 stub corrupts the x87 stack — fix is fninit+fldcw before each FP phase (documented in game.txt header)
- **Regression suite**: test_ground.py is NOT registered in run_all.py — the suite gate is broken until the coordinator adds it
