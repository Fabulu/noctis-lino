      ( *** w5shade - does shade() take its destination buffer? ***

	NOCTIS-0.CPP:1151 is

	    void shade (unsigned char far *palette_buffer,
	                unsigned first_color, unsigned num_of_colors,
	                float start_r, float start_g, float start_b,
	                float finish_r, float finish_g, float finish_b)

	and 14 of its 21 call sites pass surface_palette, not tmppal.
	A port whose shade() can only write tmppal cannot express the
	majority of the original's calls.

	So this probe simply asks for the thing the original asks for:
	it points [SHdstb] at srfpal6 and runs a shade.  If the library
	has no such parameter the build fails with "symbol not declared",
	and THAT IS THE RESULT - test_wave5.py reports the build failure
	as the check failing, not as a broken harness.  If it does build,
	the probe dumps both buffers and the test requires srfpal6 to
	carry the ramp and pal6 to be untouched.

	Record 1: kind 2, 768 units, srfpal6.                             )
