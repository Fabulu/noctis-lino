The unsigned-fold control is the informative one. It does not
collapse to zero, and it should not: a wrong-but-plausible
galaxy still puts stars near the origin, where small |id|
catalogue entries are dense, so some ids are hit by chance.
That rate is the chance-match floor for ANY plausible
generator, and it is the number the real rate has to beat.
All three anchor tests fail under it, as they must.
